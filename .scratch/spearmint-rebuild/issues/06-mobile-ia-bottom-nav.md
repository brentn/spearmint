## Question

Old Spearmint has no bottom nav (top navbar + gear menu); Peppermint has a desktop-style hamburger top nav. Neither matches mint.com's mobile bottom-tab convention. What should the new bottom-nav tab set be, and is Trends/graphs (an old, never-finished stub in Spearmint) in scope for this rebuild?

## Answer

**Four tabs: Overview, Budgets, Transactions, Settings.** Accounts management lives under Settings, not as its own tab.

**No Trends/graphs** — explicitly deferred (see Out of scope on the map). Budget progress bars already cover the "am I on track" need; a dedicated charting feature is separate scope.
