# Budget status & rollup computation

Budget status is conveyed entirely through a three-state (normal/warning/over) progress-bar color — no separate notification (see [Notification scope](0007-notification-scope.md)). Expense/transfer categories are green up to and including 101% of budget, amber from there through 110%, red beyond that — a small overspend cushion before the bar turns alarming (originally a flat 85% warning threshold; widened once users found the bar landing on amber too early to be actionable). Income categories invert the over/under logic (going over target is good) and keep their own separate threshold, unaffected by the expense change. A category's `Rollover` from the prior period counts toward its percent-used calculation.

A category whose actual total goes negative (e.g. a refund/reversal exceeding other activity) reverses the bar to fill from the opposite edge instead of clamping to an invisible 0% — sized by the magnitude of the negative amount. The existing state logic already lands on the right color for this case with no separate rule: a negative expense total falls well under the green ceiling above, and a negative income total falls well under its own warning threshold (red).

A reversed bar's rendered width caps at 90% rather than 100%, regardless of true magnitude — a fully-filled track has no bare pixel left to show which edge the fill grew from, so a -100% reversed bar and a +100% forward bar would otherwise be pixel-identical. The guaranteed 10% gap this leaves is where a directional chevron (colored to match the bar's state) renders once the true magnitude exceeds the cap, with the percentage label moving next to it wherever a label already exists. This cap and its chevron apply only to the reversed direction — forward (over-budget) bars keep rendering at a full 100% cap exactly as before.

Rollup is a full-tree recursive combine: every category's spent/amount/rollover always includes all of its descendants', regardless of whether those descendants have their own budget. A parent category with no explicit budget but at least one budgeted descendant gets a synthesized `Implied Budget` row. The Budgets screen's overall month total sums only top-level rows, real or implied, to avoid double-counting a child's spend under both its own row and its parent's.

This supersedes the original rebuild-era design, which stopped rollup at the first budgeted descendant specifically to avoid double-counting. That version shipped, then GitHub issue #15 replaced it with the current full-tree combine once the parent-only default view (issue #12) exposed a problem: a budgeted child with no budgeted parent would vanish from the default "Spending by category" list entirely, since no row existed for it to roll up into.
